Nvidia RTX 5090, RTX 5080 and RTX 5070 at MSRP: How PAX West VPA Access Works
NVIDIA’s GeForce RTX 5090 Founders Edition launched with a US MSRP of $1,999, but finding a card at that price has been difficult during periods of limited supply. In September 2026, NVIDIA’s Verified Priority Access program offered selected buyers an opportunity to purchase RTX 50-series Founders Edition cards at MSRP during PAX West in Seattle. That event has ended, so buyers should check current retailer listings and NVIDIA’s official purchasing options rather than assume event pricing is still available.
